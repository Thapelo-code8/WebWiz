Our Mission Statement

We the WEB WIZARDS aim to bring awareness to Online Scams that broad online users fall prey to. Online scammers mostly target tertiary students as they are very much gullible, These scammers scam students by advertising a poster telling students that they can help them with online applications or that they can help them with universities online registrations, since students seem to be desperate for assistance they fall prey to the scheme, some scammers duplicate online commerce websites namely TEMU, SHEIN, ALI EXPRESS, ONLINE FOOD WEBSITES AND GADGETS WEBSITES, they also create fake marketplace accounts on sites like WHATSAPP AND FACEBOOK to lure gullible students and rob them of their money This issue matters in ICT because scam is like a deadly virus which has been here for ages and can't seem to go away and it is mostly connected to the digital world. Hence why we the WEB WIZARDS have created a website which is going to educate people about online scams and which is going to help them identify these online scams.
